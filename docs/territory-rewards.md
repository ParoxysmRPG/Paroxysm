Territory Rewards

Every world territory grants its controlling faction the reward shown by
territory info (name). Bonuses apply to members everywhere, not only while
inside the territory. The controlling organizations are the actual holders of
the core faction foothold and both society footholds. Support percentages do
not grant or remove the direct bonus.

Allies and affiliates without a foothold do not share control. Your permanent
core and society memberships count,
including a legacy society, even while deployed with another group.

Different named bonuses combine. Each named bonus applies only once, even
if your faction controls several territories granting it. Losing control or
leaving the faction removes the ongoing benefit; your purchased stats are
unchanged. Each territory grants at most one reward: one bonus or one potion reward.
The three potion territories grant no additional bonus. Sixteen territories
are intentionally blank. Paris appears twice in the saved directory but
represents the same named reward. Other named rewards are unique.
Skill bonuses change effective stats only. Forensics and Protection Detail
grant at least rank 2 rather than stacking onto an already purchased rank.
Territory skill bonuses respect stat deactivation and supernatural suppression.
Scheme defenses use the author's current memberships and territory control,
including while offline. Roll bonuses are additive, not success percentages.
Shanghai waives fees for direct investigations and numeric scheme research
orders; it does not waive fees for researching a person.
Istanbul discounts the normal thwart fee, rounded up. Astana discounts both
normal and limited launches; thwart fees use the actual launch cost paid.
Khartoum extends the shared cooldown after a failed thwart to seven hours.

NPC operations:
The scheduler keeps at least two NPC operations booked for each of the next
seven days, subject to an eligible NPC faction, unlocked territory and free
operation time slots. It runs hourly, preserves existing bookings, and saves
new operations. Hours 7 and 17 are reserved. Times use the game's clock.
Use operation list and operation signup (number) to view and join operations.
On startup it also fills today where future time slots remain. Server
downtime, the Cleanse and an overlapping active battle can prevent an
operation from launching.
